# Management TX restart after ring backpressure

Base: 9e3540d92b4e9ff4e3e012c0739af83912ae1f6a. COV-RTL-014/009 remain IN_PROGRESS.

## Evidence and change

Frozen NetBSD if_schedule_deferred_start returns when the Ethernet if_snd is
empty. Management frames reside in ic_mgtq. A full MGNT or BE ring makes the
producer set IFF_OACTIVE before dequeuing, so management frames can remain
queued across reclamation without any Ethernet traffic to trigger a restart.

Completion now samples ic_mgtq at splnet under producer_lock after checking
enabled/RUNNING and clearing OACTIVE. Pending management takes if_start_lock
after producer_lock is released. Data-only completion keeps the initialized
deferred path. The normal producer checks enabled/RUNNING/OACTIVE again under
its own lock; a stop between wake selection and restart cannot reopen TX.
An empty queue by restart time is harmless. If another ring is still full,
the producer retains its existing backpressure check and leaves packets queued.

## Lock and lifetime source proof

1. Native IRQ establishes SOFTINT_NET without SOFTINT_MPSAFE; completion is
   dispatched by that softint, never the hard interrupt.
2. rtwn8723be_dp_tx_done drops dp->tx_lock before invoking completion.
3. Completion drops producer_lock before if_start_lock can reenter TX; splnet
   covers queue inspection and restart. if_start_lock supplies the framework's
   kernel-lock convention. No private ifnet deferred structure is accessed.
4. Successful runtime_shutdown closes the producer, stops the datapath and
   disestablishes IRQ/softint before ring reset. Runtime unregister retains the
   owner on failed stop. Thus this synchronous callback does not create another
   asynchronous lifetime beyond the existing IRQ rundown.
5. The frozen native rtwn_softintr likewise restarts at splnet after reclamation.
